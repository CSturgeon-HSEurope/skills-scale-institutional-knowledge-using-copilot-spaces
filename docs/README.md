# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core project management guidance for OctoAcme. The processes are designed to move work from an initial idea to a release-ready outcome with clear ownership, measurable goals, and continuous improvement. OctoAcme begins with project initiation, where the business need, stakeholders, success metrics, timeline, resource needs, and early risks are validated before work is approved. Once a project is aligned, the team moves into planning to turn the initiative into a prioritized backlog, estimate effort, define dependencies, and capture the Definition of Done and release milestones.

During execution, teams work in small, reviewable increments and track progress through a shared project board, standups, and milestone reviews. The project management model emphasizes accountability across roles: the Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, dependencies, and communication; the Product Manager owns outcomes and prioritization; developers deliver implementation and testing; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide approvals and business input. Risk management is active throughout the lifecycle, with issues documented, mitigation plans assigned, and blockers escalated through defined paths when needed.

Communication is a central part of the OctoAcme model. Teams hold regular standups and weekly delivery or PM/Product syncs, share status through a single source of truth, and use demos and stakeholder updates to keep work visible and aligned. Quality assurance is built into delivery and release, with required unit and integration tests, acceptance criteria, review gates, and passing CI and security scans before work is considered ready to merge or deploy. Releases include staging smoke tests, rollback planning, and post-deployment verification, while retrospectives capture lessons learned and drive ongoing improvements through tracked action items.
