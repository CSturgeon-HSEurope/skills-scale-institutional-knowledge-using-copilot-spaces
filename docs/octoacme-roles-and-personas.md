# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers understand user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and share findings with the team
- Create and validate user flows, prototypes, and interface designs
- Identify usability and accessibility needs and include them in design decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to refine problems, requirements, and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers and Tech Leads to assess feasibility and resolve design questions
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility validation
- Align research and design milestones and dependencies with Project Managers

---

## Tech Leads and Software Architects

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Software Architects guide technical direction and help ensure solutions are secure, reliable, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define or guide architecture and technical standards for the project
- Facilitate engineering decisions and communicate technical trade-offs
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration needs
- Support Developers with implementation guidance and design reviews

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to plan and deliver implementation
- Explain feasibility, technical risks, and trade-offs to Product Managers
- Coordinate technical dependencies and risk responses with Project Managers
- Partner with QA/Testing to ensure designs are testable

---

## QA Leads and Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Test Engineers coordinate quality activities and provide evidence that the solution meets its requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test approach and identify coverage across unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Review requirements and acceptance criteria for testability
- Coordinate validation, report defects, and communicate quality risks
- Confirm test results and readiness evidence for release

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to plan tests, reproduce defects, and verify fixes
- Align acceptance criteria and expected behavior with Product Managers
- Coordinate test timing, dependencies, and quality status with Project Managers
- Collaborate with UX/UI Designers and Researchers on usability and accessibility checks

---

## DevOps/SRE and Release Managers

### Role Summary
DevOps/SRE and Release Managers support delivery pipelines and operational readiness so changes can be deployed and operated reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain or coordinate build, deployment, and release processes
- Plan monitoring, alerting, rollback, and operational verification
- Identify reliability and deployment risks and communicate readiness
- Coordinate incident response and operational handoffs when applicable

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers and Tech Leads on pipeline, infrastructure, and reliability needs
- Coordinate deployment verification and release checks with QA/Testing
- Align release timing, dependencies, and operational risks with Project Managers
- Consult Product Managers on customer impact and release constraints

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise the team on protecting systems, data, and users throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements and review plans for handling data
- Assess risks and recommend proportionate mitigations
- Coordinate or advise on security and privacy reviews and testing
- Escalate significant concerns through the appropriate incident or risk process

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to clarify security and privacy requirements
- Advise Developers and Tech Leads on secure design and implementation
- Coordinate security and privacy validation with QA/Testing
- Help Project Managers track risks, mitigations, review dependencies, and escalations

---

## Data and Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help the team measure product outcomes and ensure the data used for decisions is meaningful and reliable.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into instrumentation and measurement needs
- Advise on event definitions, data quality, and reporting
- Validate that metrics can be interpreted and used to assess outcomes
- Share post-release insights and measurement limitations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align success metrics and product questions with Product Managers
- Work with Developers and Tech Leads to implement instrumentation
- Coordinate measurement dependencies and reporting with Project Managers
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to verify tracking and data behavior

---

## Customer Support and Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives bring customer-facing and service-delivery knowledge into planning and release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer issues, support needs, and operational constraints
- Identify documentation, training, and service-readiness needs
- Help prepare customer communications and known-issue guidance
- Gather post-release feedback and route issues to the appropriate owners

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Share customer needs and feedback with Product Managers
- Coordinate documentation, training, and handoffs with Project Managers
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to understand known issues and verification steps
- Partner with DevOps/SRE or Release Managers on operational readiness and incident communications

---

## Applying Roles to a Project
- Select roles based on project scope, risk, and team needs; not every project requires a dedicated person for every role.
- A team member may cover multiple roles when appropriate, but make ownership and decision-making responsibilities explicit.
- Bring relevant roles into planning early so requirements, dependencies, testing, security, measurement, and operational handoffs are not discovered late.
